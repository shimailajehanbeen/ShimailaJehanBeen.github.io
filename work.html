Featured Project

Work that demonstrates the approach

Legal Technology · Version Control · Contract Management

Contract Drafting System via GitHub

A working proof-of-concept that applies software engineering principles — specifically Git version control — to the problem of contract management. Every clause change is tracked, every revision is documented, and the full history of a contract is permanently auditable.

This isn't an academic exercise. It solves a real problem: most legal teams have no meaningful version history for their contracts. This system changes that.

View Live Project ↗

What this demonstrates

  • Clause-level tracking — every change is attributed and timestamped
  • Full revision history across the contract lifecycle
  • Collaborative editing with conflict resolution
  • Structured document management without expensive CLM software
  • Technical fluency applied to a genuine legal operations problem

Why it matters: The ability to think about legal problems through a technology lens — and then build something — is rare. This project is evidence of that.
